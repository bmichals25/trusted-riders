## Design Context

### Users
TrustedRiders chaperones/operators use this app during non-emergency medical transport work, often while mobile, under time pressure, and in bright or distracting conditions. Their primary job is to understand today's ride obligations, accept incoming requests, coordinate with dispatch, and keep location telemetry flowing.

### Brand Personality
Trusted, operational, vigilant. The interface should feel calm, authoritative, and fast to scan rather than playful or decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the project's "Vigilant Command Center" language: high-end editorial hierarchy, tight radii, tonal layering, restrained color, and strong glance recognition. Light mode is primary. Dark navy surfaces are reserved for hero or primary operational context; yellow and red are semantic, not decorative.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize 0.5-second recognition for current obligations and status.
- Keep logistics dense but orderly, with left/right alignment instead of centered consumer-app layouts.
- Use tonal surfaces and spacing for hierarchy instead of heavy borders or nested cards.
- Preserve native iOS behaviors where possible, especially navigation and persistent controls.
- Make empty and low-data states useful without bloating the screen with explanation.
