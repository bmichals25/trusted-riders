# TrustedRide Certified Onboarding PDF Audit

Audit target: `docs/TrustedRide-Certified-Team-Onboarding-Guide.pdf`

Rendered evidence: `docs/onboarding-guide-rendered/page-1.png` through `page-5.png`

Generated by: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py`

## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Fail.** The guide is not generic AI slop in color or brand usage, but it still has layout tells that make it feel machine-composed rather than professionally typeset.

- Page 3 has a clear content collision: the location phone screenshot overlaps the subtitle.
- Page 2 has a stacked-phone collision: the upper phone caption sits too close to, and is partially crowded by, the lower phone frame.
- Pages 4 and 5 use repeated same-size screenshot cards, which the frontend-design guidance calls out as a templated layout pattern.
- The guide relies on fixed absolute coordinates and fixed-height blocks, so copy length and font changes can break the composition.
- Several screenshots are too small to function as instructional evidence, especially on pages 4 and 5.

The artifact is close in palette and brand voice, but the composition still needs an editorial layout pass.

## Critical Issues

### 1. Page 3 Subtitle Overlaps Phone Screenshot

- Location: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:499-500`
- Severity: Critical
- Category: Responsive / Layout
- Description: The subtitle under "Enable location before the first ride" runs into the top border of the location phone screenshot. In the rendered PDF, the second subtitle line is visually intersected by the phone frame.
- Impact: This is a visible production defect. It makes the guide look unfinished and makes the subtitle harder to read.
- WCAG/Standard: WCAG 1.4.12 Text Spacing principle, plus general print legibility.
- Recommendation: Move the phone down, reduce phone height, or change the page to a two-column layout where the screenshot starts below the heading block. Add a measured `y` flow so the image starts only after the heading/subtitle height is known.
- Suggested command: `/arrange`

## High-Severity Issues

### 2. Page 2 Phone Caption Collides With Lower Phone Stack

- Location: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:491-492`, `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:336-344`
- Severity: High
- Category: Responsive / Layout
- Description: The "TESTFLIGHT INSTALL" caption is positioned under the first phone screenshot while the second phone begins almost immediately below it. The caption reads as trapped between two device frames.
- Impact: The page feels crowded and the caption hierarchy is unclear. It also creates a fragile layout where any font or image size change can turn into true overlap.
- WCAG/Standard: WCAG 1.4.8 Visual Presentation, readability.
- Recommendation: Put both phone screenshots in separate labeled modules, or show only one phone on this page and move sign-in to a dedicated page/section.
- Suggested command: `/arrange`

### 3. Fixed Coordinate Layout Has No Collision Guard

- Location: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:372-647`
- Severity: High
- Category: Responsive / Performance / Maintainability
- Description: Each page uses fixed x/y coordinates, fixed screenshot heights, and fixed card heights. `draw_wrapped()` returns a y position, but most major page elements do not use that result to reserve space for following content.
- Impact: The PDF can regress every time copy, font, logo dimensions, QR label, or screenshot dimensions change. This is the root cause of the visible overlaps.
- WCAG/Standard: Robustness and readable reflow expectations; not a direct WCAG web rule, but it affects accessibility of the generated document.
- Recommendation: Introduce a simple vertical flow helper per column, page-safe block objects, and a post-render collision check. Treat every block as measured before drawing the next.
- Suggested command: `/harden`

### 4. Screenshots Are Too Small To Support Onboarding

- Location: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:360-369`, `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:543-620`
- Severity: High
- Category: Accessibility / Usability
- Description: Pages 4 and 5 place full app screenshots inside 160-point-wide cards. Much of the actual app text is unreadable in the final PDF.
- Impact: Team members cannot reliably follow the app using the screenshots. The guide promises example screenshots, but the screenshots become decorative rather than instructional.
- WCAG/Standard: WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast and 1.4.4 Resize Text by practical analogy; content must remain legible.
- Recommendation: Use one large screenshot per workflow section with numbered callouts, or split pages so screenshots can be at least 240-280 points tall and paired with concise labels.
- Suggested command: `/onboard`

### 5. Repeated Screenshot Card Grid Feels Templated

- Location: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:360-369`, `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:543-620`
- Severity: High
- Category: Anti-pattern / Theming
- Description: Pages 4 and 5 use a repeated three-card grid with title, paragraph, and image. The frontend-design skill flags identical card grids as a common AI-generated layout tell.
- Impact: The guide loses the app's calm operational tone and starts to feel like a generic product brochure.
- WCAG/Standard: Not a WCAG violation; this is a brand and design-quality issue.
- Recommendation: Replace the grid with an operator workflow layout: one dominant phone screenshot, a right-side action checklist, and a compact "when to use this" row.
- Suggested command: `/normalize`

### 7. App Font Matching Is Partial

- Location: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:60-69`, `lib/theme.ts:106-120`
- Severity: Medium
- Category: Theming
- Description: The PDF embeds Helvetica Neue, which is close to iOS/system typography, but the app's tokenized type scale uses smaller operational sizes and very strong section kickers. The PDF H1s are much larger than the app's command-style hierarchy.
- Impact: The PDF feels adjacent to the app, not fully of the app.
- WCAG/Standard: Design system consistency.
- Recommendation: Reduce H1 sizes, use tighter operational section labels, and mirror the app's body/footnote scale from `lib/theme.ts`.
- Suggested command: `/typeset`

### 8. Callout Copy Is Too Dense For Fixed Boxes

- Severity: Medium
- Category: Accessibility / UX Writing
- Description: Callout boxes contain long explanatory paragraphs in small text. They fit now, but only because font size is reduced.
- Impact: The warning content is important, yet it is harder to scan than the main bullets.
- WCAG/Standard: WCAG 3.1.5 Reading Level by practical principle; plain language and scannability.

### 9. Generated Phone Mockups Have Oversized Radii And Consumer-App Feel

- Location: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:134-218`
- Severity: Medium
- Category: Theming / Anti-pattern
- Description: Synthetic screens use large 18-22 px card radii and centered consumer onboarding layouts. The project design rules prefer tight radii, left/right alignment, and operational glance recognition.
- Impact: The guide's example screens do not fully match the app's "Vigilant Command Center" design language.
- WCAG/Standard: Design system consistency.
- Recommendation: Make synthetic screenshots look more like the actual app: tighter radii, less centered copy, stronger operational labels.
- Suggested command: `/normalize`

### 10. No Automated Visual Regression Check

- Location: `tools/build-onboarding-guide.py:650-661`
- Severity: Medium
- Category: Performance / Maintainability
- Description: The generator creates the PDF but does not render pages and assert non-overlap, page bounds, or minimum margins as part of the build.
- Impact: Layout defects can ship even after a successful script run.
- WCAG/Standard: Quality engineering best practice.
- Recommendation: Add a `--qa` path that renders pages and checks known danger zones or compares against approved snapshots.
- Suggested command: `/harden`

## Patterns & Systemic Issues

- Absolute layout is the main systemic problem. The guide needs measured blocks, not hand-placed rectangles.
- Screenshots are being treated as decorative card content instead of the primary onboarding evidence.
- The visual language uses the right colors, but not always the right operational hierarchy.
- Warning/caveat copy is taking too much room inside boxes instead of being promoted to a clean troubleshooting flow.
- The PDF lacks an automated post-render QA step, so overlap can reappear silently.

## Positive Findings

- The guide uses the correct core brand colors from the app token family: navy, operational blue, amber/yellow for caution, and light tonal surfaces.
- The TrustedRide Certified and MuseLabs logos are present and legible.
- The public TestFlight caveat is honest and useful.
- The content covers the right onboarding jobs: install, sign in, location, current ride, messages, schedule, and settings.
- The rendered PDF is 5 pages, which is a reasonable length for team distribution.

## Recommendations By Priority

1. Immediate: Fix page 3 overlap and page 2 phone-stack collision.
2. Short-term: Replace repeated screenshot card grids with fewer, larger instructional screenshots and numbered callouts.
3. Medium-term: Convert the PDF generator from fixed coordinates to measured vertical flow helpers.
4. Medium-term: Align typography more closely with `lib/theme.ts`, especially body size, section kickers, and heading scale.
5. Long-term: Add an automated render-and-audit step that catches overlap before distribution.

## Suggested Commands For Fixes

- `/arrange`: Fix physical layout, page rhythm, screenshot placement, and collisions.
- `/typeset`: Reduce oversized headings, tighten body hierarchy, and ensure the PDF consistently uses the app-like font stack.
- `/normalize`: Bring generated examples and repeated cards closer to the TrustedRide design system.
- `/onboard`: Improve the actual onboarding flow by making screenshots larger and steps more task-oriented.
- `/harden`: Add generator safeguards and visual QA checks so future edits do not reintroduce overlap.
